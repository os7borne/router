## What's new
- Router now updates itself. Check from the gear menu or Settings → Updates.
- A simpler popup: the status card and network list collapse while your connection is good, and open when it isn't.
- Signals, Live and Tools are combined into one Diagnostics tab.
- Browsing, video calls, streaming, gaming and downloads are rated for your current connection.
- A save icon on every network. Networks you don't save are forgotten after a few days.
- A warning on public networks, with Tailscale controls.
- A sign-in window for café and hotel Wi-Fi that fills in your saved details and stops at one-time codes.
